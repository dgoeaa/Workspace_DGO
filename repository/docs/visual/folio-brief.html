Sheet 1 · The promise

What began as a tracker is now the Agency's operating record.

The platform started as a way to follow correspondence through the DG's office. It has grown into a full operating estate: a staff platform with 29 workspaces, a public portal, executive briefing and meeting management, an administration suite, and a flow engineering bench. Three ideas hold it together.

One record

A letter handed in at the counter, an email to customer service, a portal submission and a DG directive all become the same correspondence record. The channel is a field, not a separate system.

One owner per action

Every step that changes a record belongs to exactly one workspace. An action nobody owns is refused before it runs, so the process cannot drift from what was agreed.

One trail

Each change is audited, receipted and safe to retry. Work entered offline is queued and sent when the connection returns. Nothing is lost between hands.

"The platform must be judged by whether a user can receive or locate a matter from any ingestion source, register it, minute it, assign it, track it, review it, dispatch it, close it and archive it without confusion."The product definition's own test

Sheet 5 · The governance spine

Accountability is built into the structure.

Four layers, and data flows down through them. The governance layer sits between every screen and every system of record, so no workspace can change a record without passing through it.

Owned actions

An action that is not declared and owned throws instead of executing.

Audited

Every attempt is logged with its full detail. The operator sees a plain message, never raw error text.

Idempotent

A double-click or a retry never creates a second task or a second dispatch.

Receipted

The receipt ledger records what the back end confirmed, not what the screen assumed.

Presentation

Shell and workspaces

Web-component shell, hash router, lazy-loaded workspaces, three themes.

Governance

Action authority

Ownership, audit log, idempotency, receipt ledger, offline queue, one-time-code step-up.

Integration

Endpoint registry

Contract resolution, redaction, retry, de-duplication and queueing for every call.

Back end

Power Automate and SharePoint

25 contract keys, document libraries, and identity by one-time code.

A proof, not a claim: 108 governance assertions run without a browser in about a second.

Sheet 7 · The engineering bench

The Agency can run and maintain the platform in-house.

A platform the Agency depends on has to be operable by its own IT department. Alongside the workspaces sits a full set of instruments for seeing, testing and repairing the connections underneath.

Admin Suite

One interface replacing five screens and a drawer of standalone consoles: endpoint estate, flow catalogue, commissioning, capsule registry, people and platform settings.

Endpoint Console

Every contract key on both sides of the wire, with its state, its probe result and what calls it. Wiring is a configuration task done here, not a code change.

Sheet 8 · Built for here

Designed for how the Agency actually works.

Unreliable power and connectivity, mixed devices, and a small in-house team were treated as design inputs from the start.

Offline durable

Actions taken without a connection are queued in the browser and sent, once, when it returns.

Zero build

No bundler, no framework, nothing from a CDN. Edit a file, refresh the page. Any web server can host it.

Configuration over code

Routes, roles, endpoints, ownership and workflow visibility live in declarative configuration files.

Accessible

Skip link, landmarks, live regions, focus-trapped drawers, and a forced-colours high-contrast theme.

Sheet 9 · For whom

What each part of the Agency gains.

DG/CEO

Decisions without the paper chase

A correspondence and decision hub showing what waits on the DG, what has been decided and where each item goes next. Briefs and meeting outcomes arrive in the same place.

Office of the DG

Minutes that stay attached

Registry minutes, assignments and follow-ups are recorded against the matter itself. An enquiry is answered by looking it up, not by asking around.

Heads of department

Workload you can see

Acknowledgement queues, deadlines, FastTrack escalation of late work and approvals, with a record of who confirmed what and when.

Staff

One list of my work

My Work collects every task routed to a person, whatever channel it came from, with an assistant that answers "where has this got to?" from records they may see.

The public and MDAs

A receipt and a reference

Submissions acknowledged immediately and trackable at any hour, which cuts calls and visits to the registry.

Audit and IT

Evidence by default

An audit log, a receipt ledger and closure evidence in the archive. The in-house team can run the estate with its own instruments.

Sheet 10 · The road to live

Going live is a configuration step. The code is done.

What stands between the platform and production sits in the tenant and with decision-makers. Each remaining item names the person who can close it.

Built

Platform and portal

Workspaces, governance spine, portal and bench in place, guarded by an automated quality gate.

Piloted

Wired and exercised

Endpoints wired against the tenant and the pilot commissioning pass completed.

Next

Switch identity on

Authentication is fully provisioned and deliberately off. Activation is one setting plus two one-time-code flows. No identity provider tenant is needed.

Then

Production acceptance

Formal evidence, security sign-off and governance closure through the production readiness register.

End of folio

One record, one owner for each step, and a trail anyone can follow.
