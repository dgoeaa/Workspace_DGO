What provisioning creates, and where it puts it

Two provisioning scopes, read from the two contracts that govern them: the ten governance lists the platform reads on every call, and the seven HTTP flow registry lists that would hold the estate’s own flow truth. Every list, every field, every seed, every call — searchable, addressable, and rendered exactly as the contract states it.

Defined is not created

The registry contract is a reading of a flow definition, not of a run. Every one of its 102 field rows records Defined; actual validation outcome unavailable. A row on this page means the flow is configured to create that resource — never that the resource exists.

The governance contract is the opposite case and says so: all ten lists exist, it updates them in place, and it never creates a list — because creating by title is what left seventeen duplicates in the tenant.
