## Provisioning state — BLOCKED-ON-PROVISIONING

The three resources below do not exist in the tenant. `tests/http-flow-registry.test.mjs` asserts that none of the seven governance lists appears in the capture of https://nitdanigeria.sharepoint.com/sites/DGO_ECM_GOVERNANCE, and this package cannot be pasted until they are built and their GUIDs recorded in `docs/folio-Master-Flows-and-Endpoints/deployment/governance/list-bindings.json`. While a GUID is missing the actions target the list by **title**, which the estate's convention does not permit — that is the visible marker of the blocked state, not a decision.

| Resource | Kind | Unique key | GUID |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DGO_HTTPFlowRegistry` | List | `RegistryKey` | **not provisioned** |
| `DGO_HTTPFlowExecutionLedger` | List | `ExecutionKey` | **not provisioned** |
| `DGO_HTTPFlowArtefacts` | DocumentLibrary | — | **not provisioned** |

The unique keys are not decoration. `ExecutionKey` unique on `DGO_HTTPFlowExecutionLedger` is what makes this
scope idempotent under a lost race: the read-before-write below is an optimisation, and SharePoint
is the guard. `RegistryKey` unique on `DGO_HTTPFlowRegistry` is what stops two concurrent runs
from creating two registry rows for one flow.

## Variables the host flow must declare

Logic Apps accepts `InitializeVariable` only at a workflow's top level, so a clipboard package
cannot declare them — paste `DGO_FLOW_TRUTH_PERSISTENCE.variables.designer-paste.json` at the
top of the flow first, or confirm the flow already carries the estate's standard block.

| Variable | Type | Why |
