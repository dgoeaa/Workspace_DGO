Privacy notice

How this portal uses your information.

This notice explains what the NITDA Document Portal collects when you submit a document, track a request or open a helpdesk case, why it is collected, where it is kept and what you can ask us to do with it. It is given under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Last updated 20 October 2026.

Who is responsible

The National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA), No. 28 Port Harcourt Crescent, Off Gimbiya Street, Area 11, Garki, Abuja, decides how personal data collected through this portal is used. Questions about this notice, and requests about your data, go to the Agency's data protection officer at dpo@nitda.gov.ng.

What we collect, and when

  • When you submit a document: your full name, official email address, organisation and organisation type; your telephone number and state, if you give them; the type of correspondence and its priority; the subject and the purpose you write; and the files you attach, with the file names you sent.
  • When you confirm your email address: the address, and a one-time code we email to it. The code expires and can be used once.
  • When you track a request: the tracking ID, and either the email address it was submitted under or the confirmation from a one-time code.
  • When you open a helpdesk case: the topic, your name, your email address, the tracking ID if you give one, and your message.
  • When you respond on a request: the response or note you send, attached to that request.

The portal does not ask for your national identity number, bank details or date of birth, and you do not create an account.

Why we use it

  • To receive, register and route your correspondence to the unit that handles it, and to reply to you.
  • To send you the acknowledgement, status changes and the outcome by email.
  • To confirm that you control the email address before we accept a submission or show you a record.
  • To answer helpdesk cases.
  • To keep the service secure and working: each request to the Agency's systems leaves a run record, which is used to detect failures and misuse.

We use this information to carry out the Agency's public functions under the National Information Technology Development Agency Act 2007. For a helpdesk case we also rely on the consent you give in the form, which you can withdraw at any time.

Where it is kept, and who sees it

Submissions, attachments, helpdesk cases and confirmation codes are held in the Agency's Microsoft 365 environment, in SharePoint registers and document libraries, and are processed by workflows the Agency operates there. Microsoft 365 is a cloud service, so your data may be stored in Microsoft data centres outside Nigeria, under the Agency's agreement with Microsoft. Emails to you are sent from the Agency's own mailboxes.

Registry officers, and staff of the unit your correspondence is routed to, can see your submission. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing. We disclose it to others only where the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

Your submission becomes part of the Agency's registry and is kept under the registry's retention rules. Official correspondence is kept for seven years from the date of the record unless its records class sets a different period. One-time codes expire shortly after they are sent and can be used only once.

What is stored on your device

To work without an account, the portal keeps a few things in your browser's local storage on this device only: a draft of a submission you have not finished, the tracking IDs of requests sent from this browser, anything waiting to be sent while you were offline, and your theme choice. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

The portal sets no cookies of its own and loads no analytics, advertising or social-media scripts. The company that hosts these pages sets one cookie, named __test, to check that your browser runs JavaScript before it serves the page.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us to:

  • tell you whether we hold data about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, or restrict how we use it, where the law allows;
  • give you your data in a commonly used electronic format;
  • stop using your data where you object to it;
  • stop relying on your consent, where consent is the basis.

Write to dpo@nitda.gov.ng and quote your tracking ID or helpdesk case reference, so we can find the record quickly. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.

Keeping it secure

Pages and submissions travel over encrypted HTTPS connections. Before it accepts a submission, the portal can ask you to confirm your email address with a one-time code. Each attachment is accepted once, against an upload ticket issued for that submission and the file size you declared. A tracking lookup returns status, dates and the public timeline only, never the attachments or the name of the officer handling the request.

Changes to this notice

When this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
