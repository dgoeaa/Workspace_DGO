# Withdrawal and Quarantine Status

The 37 Version 2-only payloads are **quarantined, not adjudicated as obsolete or permanently excluded**. Their paths, sizes, and hashes are listed in `WITHDRAWAL_REGISTER.csv`; their payloads are not included.

Each requires one authorised disposition: recover, sanitize, retire, replace with a demonstrably equivalent tracked artifact, or remain pending. Engineering must not decide governance disposition merely to make a test pass.
